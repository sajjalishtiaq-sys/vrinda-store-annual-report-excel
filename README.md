# Vrinda Store Annual Report (Excel)

An Excel data analysis project that turns a year of Vrinda Store sales data into an interactive dashboard and a written insights report. Built with cleaned data, Pivot Tables, charts, and slicers.

- **Orders vs Sales** by month
- **Sales: Men vs Women**
- **Order Status** (Delivered, Returned, Refunded, Cancelled)
- **Top 5 states** by sales
- **Orders by Age group and Gender**
- **Orders by sales channel**
- **Highest selling category**

## Key Insights

- **Women drive sales:** Women account for about 64% of sales versus 36% for men, and they lead in every age group. Adult women are the biggest buyers.
- **Sales decline through the year:** Orders and sales are highest in the early months and fall steadily toward December.
- **Delivery rate is high:** About 92% of orders were delivered. Returns, cancellations, and refunds make up the remaining ~8%.
- **Top states:** Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh lead in sales.
- **Top channels:** Amazon (36%), Myntra (23%), and Flipkart (22%) together bring in roughly 81% of sales.
- **Best category:** Sets make up about half of all sales (49.6%), followed by Kurta (23.4%) and Western Dress (14.9%).

## Recommendations

- Focus marketing on adult women.
- Prioritize Amazon, Myntra, and Flipkart, and stock Sets and Kurtas heavily.
- Strengthen sales in Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh.
- Reduce returns and cancellations with better sizing information and quality checks.
- Run promotions in the second half of the year to counter the sales decline.

## How to Use

1. Download `vrinda_store_analysis.xlsx`.
2. Open it in Microsoft Excel.
3. Go to the **annual report** or **Dashboard** sheet.
4. Use the slicers (Channel, Status, Month, Category) to filter the charts.
